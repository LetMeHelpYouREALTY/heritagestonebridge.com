## GBP Q&A (post as owner answers)

**Q: What is Heritage at Stonebridge?**  
A: Heritage at Stonebridge is a Lennar-built, guard-gated 55+ active adult community in Summerlin West, Las Vegas (89138). The neighborhood includes 421 single-family homes across nine floor plans in three collections.

**Q: What is the age requirement at Heritage at Stonebridge?**  
A: At least one resident must be 55 or better, consistent with HUD guidelines for active adult communities.

**Q: Is Heritage at Stonebridge guard-gated?**  
A: Yes — staffed guard-gated entry with visitor verification, not just a keypad gate.

**Q: What amenities are in the community?**  
A: An 8,000 sq. ft. clubhouse, fitness center, resort-style pool and spa, heated lap pool, pickleball and bocce courts, walking trails, and social programming.

**Q: How do I schedule a tour or consultation with Dr. Jan Duffy?**  
A: Call or text (702) 789-6561, email DrDuffySells@HeritageStonebridge.com, or book online at heritagestonebridge.com/contact.

**Q: Does Dr. Jan help sellers in Heritage at Stonebridge?**  
A: Yes. Dr. Jan lists and markets homes in Heritage and other Summerlin 55+ communities with Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Nevada Properties.

**Q: How does Heritage compare to Sun City Summerlin?**  
A: Heritage is smaller (421 homes), Lennar-built, and staff guard-gated. Sun City is larger with more golf and clubs. Dr. Jan helps you compare lifestyle, HOA, and resale fit — see heritagestonebridge.com/vs-sun-city-summerlin.

**Q: What do homes cost in Heritage at Stonebridge?**  
A: Pricing depends on plan, elevation, and resale vs. new inventory. Request a current MLS snapshot — listings commonly range from the $500s to $850,000+ depending on market conditions.
